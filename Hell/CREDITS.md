# Hell — pixel hell for angelOS's demon

Seven 16-colour pixel-art wallpapers (1920×1080, and 1080×1920 for portrait screens)
made from public-domain paintings: cropped, reduced to 1/4, pushed into one hell
palette with 4×4 Bayer dithering and scaled back up without smoothing
(`tools/hell-pixelate.py`). angelOS puts them up while the demon rules
(Settings → Y2K → Angel or demon).
